My MS Journal — your symptom diary, simple and private.

My MS Journal helps you track your symptoms daily and prepare your neurology appointments effectively. Log symptom intensity in seconds, visualize monthly trends, and share reliable data with your doctor.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Daily symptom grid: presence, absence or intensity for each symptom (fatigue, pain, visual disturbances, spasticity…).
• Relapse identification and tracking: mark critical days, note duration and recovery.
• Monthly and annual view to visualize disease evolution over time.
• Patient profile and disease-modifying therapy (DMT) tracking.
• Semantic dictionary: link your personal expressions to recognized symptoms.
• PDF export for your neurology appointments.
• 100% local AI analysis on desktop: identify your patterns without sending data anywhere.

YOUR DATA BELONGS TO YOU
• No data stored on our servers.
• Sync across your devices via YOUR private Google Drive (optional).
• No ads, no trackers, no data selling.

WHO IS IT FOR?
Anyone living with multiple sclerosis — or supporting a loved one — who wants a reliable symptom history and better communication with their neurologist.

My MS Journal is not a medical device and does not provide diagnoses. It is a personal tracking tool designed to support your relationship with a healthcare professional.
